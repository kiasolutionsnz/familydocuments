## NZ wedge

**VERIFIED FACT:** NZTA vehicle licensing/WoF workflows already produce reminders, so the product must add cross-document household context rather than merely duplicate a reminder. NZ Consumer Protection recommends keeping photographed receipts, warranties and purchase information. These create locally resonant onboarding content.

**PROPOSAL:** modular NZ pack:

- WoF, registration and driver/passport expiry extraction and reminders;
- home insurance, rates, LIM/consent, contractor invoice and warranty grouping;
- pet registration and annual household review later;
- optional links to official services, not brittle scraping.

**NEEDS RESEARCH:** LINZ/council data licensing and API terms dataset by dataset; rates and consent access varies by council. Do not represent public data as freely reusable or couple the core schema to NZ.

## Strategy verdict

**PROPOSAL:** **NZ-FIRST VALIDATION → AU/NZ PRODUCT → GLOBAL ENGLISH EXPANSION.** Build global-capable identifiers, dates, currencies, providers and policy packs from day one, but do not launch globally before proving trust, activation and 90-day retention. NZ is a learning advantage, not a durable moat.
