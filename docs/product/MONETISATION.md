# Monetisation and unit economics — Family Passport

LAST REVIEWED: 2026-08-17

RESEARCH OWNER: Software Factory market-researcher

SOURCES: Official competitor pricing and cloud-service pricing listed below

COUNTRIES CHECKED: NZ, AU, UK, US, Canada, Singapore

VERIFICATION STATUS: Competitor pricing verified 2026-08-17; economics are labelled estimates, not quotes

## Business model

**PROPOSAL:** freemium household subscription, no advertising.

- Free: one adult, one Google Drive connection, 25 indexed documents, limited OCR/reminders, three sourced searches/month, export/delete.
- Family: **NZ$12–15/month or NZ$120–150/year**, up to five adults, 500–1,000 indexed documents, recurring reminders, forwarding inbox, sourced retrieval and private/shared items.
- Family Plus: test **NZ$20–25/month or NZ$200–250/year** only after advanced permissions, multiple providers or premium support prove value.

Pricing is a **PROPOSAL**, benchmarked against Trustworthy US$10/$20/$40 monthly annual-billed, Everplans US$99.99/year, Prisidio US$45/$150 yearly, Rocket Money US$7–14/month and doxoPLUS US$5.99/month. NZ price elasticity requires testing; do not translate US pricing mechanically.

Premium OCR/AI should be usage-fair limits within paid plans, not confusing per-page microcharges. Affiliate/referral revenue may later fund relevant, explicitly separated utility/insurance comparisons, with no sale of document-derived data and no ranking distortion. B2B2C distribution through insurers, brokers, banks, advisers or employers may lower CAC but creates procurement, independence and data-sharing risks.

## Advertising verdict

**NO ADS.** This product is likely opened a few times per month, so display inventory is low. At **ESTIMATE** 3 sessions/family/month × 3 ad impressions × NZ$5 effective CPM, gross ad revenue is only NZ$0.045 per active family/month before fill and platform costs. Even 100,000 active families yields roughly NZ$4,500/month. Contextual targeting against passports, bills, insurance, health or estate content would undermine trust and may create unacceptable privacy risk. Trustworthy explicitly says subscribers—not user information—are its revenue source, setting a strong category expectation.

## Indicative monthly unit economics (NZD)

Assumptions: 25% paid conversion; blended paid ARPU NZ$13/month; blended ARPU across all active families NZ$3.25; an active family processes 12 new pages/month and asks 6 AI questions; cloud OCR/extraction is tiered and cached; BYOS avoids durable-original storage but OCR text, metadata, thumbnails, logs and backups remain. Costs exclude salaries, CAC, taxes, payment fees and one-time security/compliance work.

| Active families | Revenue | OCR/LLM/embeddings | DB/search/compute | notifications/email/support tooling | Total variable infra | Indicative gross margin before payment fees/support labour |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1,000 | NZ$3,250 | NZ$350–700 | NZ$600–1,100 | NZ$150–300 | NZ$1,100–2,100 | 35–66% |
| 10,000 | NZ$32,500 | NZ$3,000–6,000 | NZ$2,500–5,000 | NZ$800–1,800 | NZ$6,300–12,800 | 61–81% |
| 100,000 | NZ$325,000 | NZ$25,000–50,000 | NZ$15,000–30,000 | NZ$5,000–12,000 | NZ$45,000–92,000 | 72–86% |

**ESTIMATE:** steady-state document processing can support SaaS margins, but initial backfill, support and security/compliance dominate at small scale. A 200-page initial import could cost several dollars per family when specialised extractors/LLMs are used; therefore free backfill must be capped and cheap OCR/classification used before targeted extraction.

Sensitivity: at 10% paid conversion, blended ARPU falls to NZ$1.30 and the 1,000-family stage is structurally loss-making. At 35% paid conversion and NZ$14 paid ARPU, blended ARPU is NZ$4.90 and economics improve materially. The decisive variables are conversion, retention, backfill pages and support burden—not original-file storage.

## Commercial thresholds

- ≥20% of activated target households accept NZ$120/year in a price test or ≥10% prepay/refundable deposit.
- ≥70% gross margin forecast at 10k active families after measured document/AI use.
- 90-day retained households ≥40%; ≥30% perform a meaningful monthly action.
- Payback on CAC ≤12 months; otherwise test partnerships before paid consumer acquisition.
