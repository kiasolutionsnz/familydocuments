## Core differentiation verdict

**VERIFIED FACT:** competitors already provide most individual components. Trustworthy provides substantially the same broad outcome and naming. Open-source DMSs already provide OCR, classification, email ingestion and search.

**PROPOSAL:** BYOS + permission-filtered family entities + confidence-gated extraction + source-cited retrieval is a meaningful *technical bundle*, but not yet a proven purchase reason. BYOS has possible portability, trust and file-cost benefits, but derived OCR text/metadata still resides with the service and therefore does not establish E2EE or “we never hold your data.”

Best potential wedge: Google Drive-selected originals plus NZ/AU home-and-vehicle renewal intelligence. Weakest claim: generic “AI that organises family documents,” already crowded.
