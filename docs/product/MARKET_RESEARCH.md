# Market research — Family Passport / Family OS

LAST REVIEWED: 2026-08-17

RESEARCH OWNER: Software Factory market-researcher

SOURCES: Primary and credible sources listed below and in the factory source register

COUNTRIES CHECKED: New Zealand, Australia, United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Singapore; Ireland considered as a future English-language expansion market

VERIFICATION STATUS: DISCOVERY COMPLETE; customer demand and willingness to pay remain NEEDS RESEARCH

## Evidence legend

- **VERIFIED FACT:** directly supported by a dated source.
- **ESTIMATE:** analyst model using stated inputs; not observed demand.
- **ASSUMPTION:** unverified premise used to form a test.
- **PROPOSAL:** recommended decision or experiment.
- **NEEDS RESEARCH:** evidence was unavailable or requires customer/product testing.

## Executive finding

**PROPOSAL — VALIDATE FIRST.** There is real life-admin pain, but public evidence does not prove that mainstream families will install, continuously maintain, and pay for a dedicated application. The broad concept is already directly occupied by Trustworthy, Everplans and Prisidio, while bills, home inventory and password/legacy access have strong specialist incumbents. The defensible test is narrower: help time-poor property-owning households find an important document and prevent an expiry/renewal from being missed, while originals remain in a folder they select in Google Drive.

**VERIFIED FACT:** Trustworthy already calls itself “The Family Operating System” and currently combines hosted encrypted storage, Inbox Autopilot, Household AI/AI answers, reminders, family members, permissions and legacy access. Its annual-billed tiers are free, US$10/month, US$20/month and US$40/month. This materially disproves concept uniqueness.

## Is the customer problem painful enough?

Evidence supports the consequences, but not yet dedicated-app adoption:

- **VERIFIED FACT:** AARP warns that when wills and advance directives cannot be found after incapacity or death, loved ones scramble to assist or settle affairs; it recommends communicating where records are stored and how to access them.
- **VERIFIED FACT:** the Insurance Information Institute says an up-to-date home inventory makes claims easier and helps substantiate losses. Its 2023 US survey found only 47% of homeowners had prepared one, implying a large intention/action gap rather than guaranteed app demand.
- **VERIFIED FACT:** New Zealand Consumer Protection explicitly advises retaining receipts, contracts, warranties, manuals and purchase details, and suggests photographing receipts so proof can be found later.
- **VERIFIED FACT:** multiple paid products have persisted—Everplans (US$99.99/year), Prisidio (US$45 or US$150/year), and Trustworthy (US$120–US$480/year)—which verifies a paying niche, not its size. Reliable market share and active-family counts are **DATA NOT AVAILABLE**.

**Answer:** painful enough for a motivated segment—property owners, family administrators, caregivers and estate-preparing adults—but **NEEDS RESEARCH** for a horizontal family market. A dedicated app is most plausible when it avoids manual filing and produces recurrent action. “Peace of mind” alone is episodic and vulnerable to setup abandonment.

## Positioning and primary segment

**PROPOSAL:** lead with “Your household records, ready when life asks”—not “Family OS,” a term already used by Trustworthy and also by an unrelated family-office platform. “Family Passport” is more distinctive but can imply identity/travel documents. A better working category is **Household Records Assistant** or **Home & Family Records**; naming requires testing.

Primary customer: the adult who already performs household administration, owns or manages a home and one or more vehicles, uses Google Drive, and has a partner/family member who occasionally needs access. Initial targeting should emphasise 35–60-year-old property-owning households and adults coordinating an older parent, without initially storing health records.

Killer use case: select a Drive folder or share 10–30 files; within 10–15 minutes receive a confirmed dashboard of upcoming passport, insurance, vehicle and warranty dates and be able to retrieve the source document in seconds.

## Recurring engagement

Monthly return triggers must be legitimate and data-driven:

- newly forwarded/shared receipts and renewal notices awaiting confirmation;
- upcoming expiry/renewal/WoF/registration reminders;
- a monthly “household records changed / actions due” digest;
- property and vehicle maintenance/service events;
- warranty windows and annual insurance/home-record review;
- fast retrieval during purchase returns, travel, service bookings and claims.

**ESTIMATE:** without automated or one-step ongoing capture, median use will be quarterly or less. Bills can raise frequency but move the product into crowded financial aggregation, broader permissions and stronger trust barriers.

## Onboarding feasibility

**PROPOSAL:** 10–15 minute value is realistic only for a curated sample, not whole-drive cleanup:

1. Create adult household and explain exactly what is stored.
2. Use Google Picker to select one folder or 10–30 files.
3. OCR/classify a bounded batch; show progress and processing cost limits.
4. Present suggested people/property/vehicle links and dates.
5. Require confirmation for critical fields.
6. Generate “next 12 months” actions and demonstrate sourced search.

Success threshold: at least 70% of recruited target users reach one accurate, useful reminder or retrieval answer within 15 minutes without facilitator help. Stop/redesign below 50%.
