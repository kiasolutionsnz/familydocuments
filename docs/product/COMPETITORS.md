## Reviews and complaints

**VERIFIED FACT:** public Prisidio review samples praise accessibility, organisation and family/older-parent use, but the samples are tiny and include an employee review; they cannot establish demand or satisfaction. Trustworthy publishes testimonials, but independently verifiable representative complaint themes are **DATA NOT AVAILABLE** from the sources checked. App-store and review counts should be treated as weak reach proxies, not market share.

**PROPOSAL:** interview former/trial users of Trustworthy, Everplans and home-inventory apps. Test setup burden, reluctance to upload originals, unclear recurring value, taxonomy mismatch and family-sharing anxiety rather than assuming these weaknesses.

## Category competition

- Family vaults/legacy: **CROWDED** with differentiated distribution through advisors, employers and estate services.
- Generic storage: **HIGHLY SATURATED**; do not compete.
- Bills/subscriptions: **HIGHLY SATURATED**, frequently bundled with finance/payment products.
- Home inventory: **MODERATELY COMPETITIVE** with clear specialist products but low apparent consumer penetration.
- Provider-neutral family metadata/BYOS overlay: **UNDERSERVED technically**, but commercial demand unproven.
